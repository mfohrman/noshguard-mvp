Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026  ·  NoshGuard LLC, Buffalo Grove, IL

NoshGuard LLC ("NoshGuard," "we," "us," or "our") operates noshguard.com and provides free food recall alert services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our service.

Information We Collect

When you enroll in NoshGuard alerts, we collect:

  • Name — to personalize your alerts
  • Email address — to send you recall notifications
  • Phone number — to send you SMS recall alerts (optional)
  • Product and brand information — to match recalls to items you've purchased

We do not collect payment information, social security numbers, or government identification.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Send you FDA and USDA food recall alerts matched to products you've purchased
  • Deliver weekly recall digest emails (optional)
  • Respond to inquiries you submit through our contact forms
  • Improve the accuracy and relevance of recall matching

SMS Messaging

By providing your phone number and enrolling, you consent to receive SMS text message alerts from NoshGuard regarding food recalls affecting products you've purchased. Message frequency varies based on recall activity. Message and data rates may apply.

To opt out of SMS alerts at any time, reply STOP to any NoshGuard message. To request help, reply HELP. You can also contact us at mitch@noshguard.com.

We Never Sell Your Data

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Period.

Data Sharing

We use the following trusted service providers to operate NoshGuard:

  • Twilio — SMS delivery
  • Resend — Email delivery
  • Supabase — Secure database storage
  • NoshGuard CRM (noshguard-crm.onrender.com) — our own analytics service, operated by NoshGuard LLC. It records which pages you view on noshguard.com, the site that referred you, and your NoshGuard visitor ID. If you submit a form on our site, the email address you entered is sent to it as well, linked to that visitor ID.

These providers process your data only as necessary to deliver our service and are bound by their own privacy policies.

Cookies and Tracking

NoshGuard sets four cookies. None of them are used for advertising, and we do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers.

  • ng_vid — our own first-party visitor ID, set by NoshGuard. It stores a randomly generated identifier so we can understand how visitors move through the site: which pages are viewed, in what order, and which site referred you. The cookie itself holds only that random identifier — no name, email, or phone number. If you submit a form on our site, we send the email address you entered to our CRM alongside this identifier, as described above. Lasts about one year. Set on every page of noshguard.com.
  • ng_cid — an identifier written only if you arrive at noshguard.com through a link that carries a tracking tag, such as a link in an email we sent. It lets us connect your visit to the outreach or record that referred you, so we can tell which of our communications are working. Lasts about one year.
  • _ga — set by Google Analytics 4 to tell one visitor apart from another for aggregate traffic reporting. Lasts about two years by default.
  • _ga_HTH2FZ7TQR — set by Google Analytics 4 to keep session state for our specific analytics property. Lasts about two years by default.

The two Google Analytics cookies load only on our home page, our enrollment page, and our page for grocers. They do not load on this Privacy Policy page, on our Terms of Service page, or on our recalls page. Our own cookies, ng_vid and ng_cid, are set across the whole site.

How to opt out. Every major browser can block or delete cookies for a single site — look under "Cookies and site data" or "Privacy" in your browser's settings. Clearing ng_vid or ng_cid means we simply stop recognizing your browser between visits. To opt out of Google Analytics across every site that uses it, install Google's official browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Data Retention

We retain your enrollment information for as long as you are subscribed to NoshGuard alerts. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing mitch@noshguard.com.

Security

We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information, including encrypted database storage and secure API communications. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable precautions to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

NoshGuard is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify enrolled users of significant changes via email. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update.
