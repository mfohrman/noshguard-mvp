Privacy Policy

NoshGuard LLC · Last updated: May 2026

What We Collect

When you enroll in NoshGuard, we collect:

  • Name — to personalize your alerts
  • Email address — to send recall notifications
  • Phone number — to send SMS recall alerts
  • ZIP code (optional) — to filter alerts by your geographic area
  • Products you tell us about — to match recalls to your pantry

How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Send you food recall notifications when a recalled product matches something you may have purchased
  • Filter alerts by geography using your ZIP code
  • Improve the accuracy of our recall matching engine

We do not use your information for advertising, analytics, or any purpose other than food safety notifications.

What We Do Not Do

  • We never sell your personal information to any third party
  • We never share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or marketing companies
  • We do not track your browsing or purchasing behavior across other websites or apps
  • We do not require a loyalty card or purchase history to enroll

Third-Party Services

To deliver notifications, we use:

  • Twilio — for SMS text message delivery. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely to send you recall alerts. Twilio's privacy policy is available at twilio.com.

Recall data is sourced from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) — both public government agencies. We do not share your personal information with these agencies.

Data Security

Your information is stored securely and accessed only to deliver food safety notifications. We do not store payment information. We retain your enrollment information for as long as you are enrolled in NoshGuard.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Unsubscribe from SMS at any time by replying STOP to any text message
  • Unsubscribe from email by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email alert
  • Request deletion of your data by emailing mitch@noshguard.com
  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you by emailing mitch@noshguard.com

California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, and opt out of the sale of your personal information. NoshGuard does not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at mitch@noshguard.com.

Children's Privacy

NoshGuard is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Terms of Service

NoshGuard LLC · Last updated: May 2026

The Service

NoshGuard provides food recall notification alerts based on publicly available recall information from the FDA and USDA. The service is provided free of charge to enrolled consumers.

Accuracy and Limitations

NoshGuard alerts are based on recall information published by government agencies and our proprietary manufacturer-to-brand relationship database. Alerts may include predicted recalls where a recalled ingredient or supplier may affect a product you own — these are clearly labeled as predictions, not confirmed recalls.

NoshGuard is not a substitute for checking FDA.gov and USDA.gov directly. We recommend following instructions from official recall notices and contacting the manufacturer or retailer with questions about specific products.

No Warranty

NoshGuard provides recall alerts on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee that every applicable recall will be detected or that every alert will be delivered. Food safety decisions should not be made solely on the basis of NoshGuard alerts.

Limitation of Liability

NoshGuard LLC's liability for any claim arising from use of the service is limited to the amount you paid for the service. The NoshGuard consumer notification service is free; accordingly, NoshGuard LLC's liability to free-tier consumers is $0. This limitation does not apply where prohibited by law.
